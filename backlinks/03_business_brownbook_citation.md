Breakout® is India's leading experiential entertainment destination and Bengaluru's pioneer of interactive cinematic escape rooms. Welcoming over 243,000+ satisfied adventurers and 13,000+ verified 4.9-star reviews across four state-of-the-art facilities, Breakout specializes in live-action mystery challenges, B2B corporate team outings, HR learning & development workshops, and milestone celebrations (kids birthday parties, adult celebrations, farewells, and anniversaries).
